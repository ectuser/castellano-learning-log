# Grammar Notes

## 2026-05-17 - Lesson One Starter Block

### Pronunciation and Typing

- `h` is silent: `hola` sounds like `ola`.
- The learner may type normal `!` and `?` instead of inverted `¡` and `¿`.
- The tutor should keep showing accents and inverted punctuation in model answers, while accepting plain keyboard input when the intended form is clear.

### Ser for Identity

- `soy` = I am: `Soy Iván.`
- `eres` = you are: `Tú eres Teresa.`
- `es` = he/she/it is: `Ella es Carmen.`, `Él es Juan.`
- Subject pronouns can be omitted unless emphasis is needed: `Soy Iván.` is normal; `Yo soy Iván.` adds emphasis.

### Estar for State

- `estoy` = I am, for condition/state: `Estoy muy bien.`
- `estás` = you are, for condition/state: `¿Cómo estás?`, `¿Estás cansado?`
- Keep reviewing the contrast between answering about self with `estoy` and asking another person with `estás`.

### Articles and Adjective Agreement

- `el viaje` = the journey.
- `la hermana` = the sister.
- `bueno` agrees with masculine `el`: `El viaje es bueno.`
- `buena` agrees with feminine `la`: `La hermana es buena.`

### Bien vs Bueno/Buena

- `bien` = well: `Estoy muy bien.`
- `bueno/buena` = good: `El viaje es bueno.`, `La hermana es buena.`

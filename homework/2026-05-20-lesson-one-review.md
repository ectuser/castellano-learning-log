# Homework - 2026-05-20

Optional, small review from Lesson One. Spend about 5-7 minutes.

## 1. Write The Spanish

Write these in Spanish:

1. How is Max?
2. How is Juan's brother?
3. How is Laura's sister?
4. How was the film?
5. How was the party?
6. How is his/her sister?

## 2. Fill The Form

Choose `estoy`, `estas`, or `esta`. Accents are optional when typing, but model answers will show them.

1. I am free: `_____ libre.`
2. Are you tired?: `¿_____ cansado?`
3. My brother is ill: `Mi hermano _____ enfermo.`
4. My sister is tired: `Mi hermana _____ cansada.`

## 3. Three Sentences

Write three short Spanish sentences:

1. I am not tired.
2. My sister is not busy.
3. My brother is not ill.

## Optional Extra

Rewrite these two with the correct family word:

1. `¿Qué tal tu hermana?` -> Ask about his/her sister.
2. `Mia frato estas enfermo.` -> Say: my brother is ill.

Bring your answers to the next session. I will correct only the Lesson One targets first: `estoy/estas/esta`, `cansado/cansada`, `enfermo/enferma`, `tu/su`, `mi hermano/mi hermana`, and `¿Como esta...?` vs `¿Que tal...?`.
